# Explore versus final

When the designer requests alternatives, experiments, or options that may be discarded, use the `explore-vs-final` skill if it is installed. Build for comparison and iteration while keeping realistic content and the project's safety rules.

When a direction is selected for implementation, convert only that direction into maintainable layout, appropriate token bindings, reusable components, and complete states. Record intentional exceptions. If the decision stage is unclear and would change how the canvas is built, ask whether the result is for comparison or handoff.
